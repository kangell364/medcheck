# TIC Chapter 2210 — Texas Windstorm Insurance Association: examinable provisions

Source: Texas Insurance Code Chapter 2210, statutes.capitol.texas.gov, read
15 September 2026. Section numbers are exact; quoted phrases are verbatim.

The full chapter runs to roughly 140 pages, most of it financing machinery
(public securities, trust funds, assessments) that the blueprint does not
reach. This file records what a General Lines candidate is actually examined
on, so a lesson can be written without re-reading the whole chapter.

> **The funding structure changed in 2025.** H.B. 3689 (89th Leg.) replaced
> the Class 1/2/3 public-securities ladder with state-funded financing
> arrangements, adding Subchapters B-2, M-1 and M-2. Subchapter B-1 and much
> of Subchapter M are repealed effective **1 September 2027**, and several
> sections carry two texts — one "effective until September 01, 2027" and one
> "effective on September 01, 2027". Study material written before 2025
> describes a structure that is on its way out. Check which text is current
> before teaching the funding layers.

## What TWIA is

**§2210.001** — the association is "a residual insurer of last resort for
windstorm and hail insurance in the seacoast territory". It "shall function
in such a manner as to not be a direct competitor in the private market".

**§2210.051** — membership is compulsory: the association is composed of *all*
property insurers authorised to write property insurance in Texas, and
membership is "a condition of the insurer's authority to engage in the
business of insurance in this state".

**§2210.052** — each member participates in losses in proportion to its net
direct premiums. A new member is not assessed until the second anniversary of
joining.

## Where it applies

**§2210.003(10)** — "seacoast territory" = the first tier and second tier
coastal counties.

**§2210.003(4)** — **first tier** (14): Aransas, Brazoria, Calhoun, Cameron,
Chambers, Galveston, Jefferson, Kenedy, Kleberg, Matagorda, Nueces, Refugio,
San Patricio, Willacy.

**§2210.003(11)** — **second tier** (14): Bee, Brooks, Fort Bend, Goliad,
Hardin, Harris, Hidalgo, Jackson, Jim Wells, Liberty, Live Oak, Orange,
Victoria, Wharton.

**§2210.005** — the **commissioner** designates a catastrophe area, after at
least 10 days' notice and a hearing, on finding that windstorm and hail
insurance "is not reasonably available to a substantial number of the owners
of insurable property" there. The designation can be revoked the same way.

## Getting a policy

**§2210.202(a)** — eligibility turns on **one declination** from an insurer
authorised to write windstorm and hail coverage in the first tier counties,
after diligent effort in the voluntary market. Renewal requires evidence of
one declination **every three calendar years**.

**§2210.203(a-1)** — for structures built or altered on or after 1 September
2009 in **Zone V** or a similar high-hazard flood zone, TWIA may not issue or
renew unless the property carries **flood insurance**, where available. The
agent must offer flood coverage.

**§2210.203(b), (c)** — a one-year policy term, renewable while the property
remains insurable.

**§2210.203(c-1)** — a renewal payment **grace period of not more than 10
days** after the due date.

**§2210.2032** — TWIA must accept credit cards and must offer instalments.
**Premium financing arrangements are prohibited** (subsection (c)), because
instalments are available directly at no extra cost.

**§2210.204(d)** — if the *insured* cancels, unearned premium is refunded
**only** for: purchase of similar coverage in the voluntary market; sale of
the property to an unrelated party; total loss; or a determination that the
property is no longer insurable. Otherwise the premium is earned and not
refundable.

## What is and is not insurable

**§2210.004** — "insurable property" is property at a fixed location in a
catastrophe area, in insurable condition against windstorm and hail by normal
underwriting standards.

**§2210.210** — TWIA **may not insure a wind turbine**, whatever else the
chapter says.

**§2210.251, §2210.2515, §2210.258** — construction begun on or after 1
January 1988 must comply with the plan of operation's building code, and TWIA
generally may not insure until a **certificate of compliance** has been
issued. A structure previously insured in the private market and then
cancelled or non-renewed can be covered despite non-compliance
(§2210.258(c)), subject to conditions.

**§2210.259** — a **15% annual premium surcharge** on a policy insuring a
non-compliant residential structure; **10%** for one eligible under
§2210.258(c). The surcharge goes to the catastrophe reserve trust fund, is
not subject to premium tax or commission, and failure to pay it is failure to
pay premium.

## The coverage

**§2210.207(c)** — if the amount of insurance equals **80% or more** of full
replacement cost (or the maximum TWIA offers), coverage extends to the **full
cost of repair or replacement, without deduction for depreciation**.

**§2210.207(d)** — below 80%, liability is limited to replacement cost of the
damaged part **less depreciation**. This is the coinsurance idea in statute.

**§2210.207(e)** — the commissioner may authorise **actual cash value on the
roof covering** instead of replacement cost, with an appropriate premium
reduction and a disclosure on the face of the policy.

**§2210.208(a)** — a dwelling policy must cover **wind-driven rain "regardless
of whether an opening is made by the wind"**, loss of use, and consequential
losses. Note the contrast with standard forms, which typically require an
opening.

**§2210.209** — coverage may extend to a structure built wholly or partly
**over water**.

## Maximum liability limits (§2210.501(b))

| Property | Minimum maximum limit |
| -------- | --------------------- |
| A dwelling, including an individually owned townhouse unit, and its contents | **$350,000** |
| A building at least 75% occupied by a governmental entity | **$2,192,000** |
| Individually owned contents in an apartment, condominium or townhouse unit | **$125,000** |
| Any other structure and its contents | **$1,500,000** |

**§2210.502** — adjusted annually for inflation by reference to the **BOECKH
Index**, in $1,000 increments.

## Claims — the deadlines

**§2210.205(a)(1) and §2210.573(a)** — an insured must file a claim **not
later than the first anniversary** of the date the damage occurred.
**§2210.205(b)** — the commissioner may extend that for good cause, **up to
180 days**.

**§2210.573(d)** — TWIA must notify the claimant in writing within **60 days**
that it accepts the claim in full, accepts in part and denies in part, or
denies in full.

**§2210.5731** — an accepted claim, or the accepted portion, must be paid
within **10 days** of that notice.

**§2210.574(b)** — a claimant disputing the *amount* has **60 days** from the
notice to demand **appraisal**; §2210.574(c) allows an extra **30 days** on a
showing of good cause. The appraisal decision is **binding and not
appealable**, except on the narrow grounds in §2210.574(g) (fraud, partiality,
misconduct, an umpire exceeding their powers).

**§2210.575** — a claimant disputing a *denial* must give notice of intent to
sue, and TWIA may require **mediation or a moderated settlement conference**
first.

**§2210.577** — an action must be brought within **two years** of the denial
notice. Subsection (b): "This section is a statute of repose and controls over
any other applicable limitations period."

**§2210.5741** — under replacement-cost coverage, documentation of completed
repairs may be submitted up to **545 days** after the notice.

## What does NOT apply to TWIA

**§2210.014** — a person **may not bring a private action** against the
association under **Chapter 541 or 542**, and Chapter 542 does not apply to it
at all. A class action may be brought only by the attorney general at the
department's request.

**§2210.572** — Subchapter L-1 is the **exclusive remedy**. TWIA cannot be
held liable for DTPA damages, and generally not for additional, punitive or
penalty damages.

This is a genuine trap: everything a candidate learns about unfair claim
settlement practices under Chapter 541/542 does not give a private right of
action against TWIA.

## Governance

**§2210.102** — a board of **nine** members appointed by the commissioner:
three from the insurance industry writing windstorm in the first tier; three
residing in the first tier (at least one a licensed P&C agent who is not a
captive agent); three from outside any catastrophe area.

**§2210.103** — three-year staggered terms, maximum three consecutive full
terms (nine years).

**§2210.016(c)** — an agent who sells a TWIA policy **is not an agent of the
association**.

## Funding, in outline

Post-2025 (Subchapter B-2): losses beyond premium are paid from reserves and
the **catastrophe reserve trust fund** (§2210.082(a)), then from a
**state-funded financing arrangement** of up to **$1 billion**
(§2210.082(b)), then from **member assessments** up to **$1 billion**
(§2210.083). Members **may not recoup an assessment** through a premium
surcharge or tax credit (§2210.083(c)).

**§2210.453(b)** — TWIA must maintain total available loss funding of at least
the **1-in-50-year** probable maximum loss.
